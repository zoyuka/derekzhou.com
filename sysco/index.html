Sysco Trace

Which foodservice operators can be shown, from public records, to buy from Sysco — and how strongly.

This is not a complete list, and it cannot be. Sysco serves roughly 700,000 customer locations and treats its customer list as a trade secret. No public directory exists. This tool infers the relationship from public documents and scores its own confidence. Absence of evidence here is not evidence that a restaurant avoids Sysco — most customers leave no public trace at all.

    How a verdict is reached

    Each operator starts at a base rate for its segment, not at zero and not at fifty-fifty. Every piece of evidence multiplies the odds by a likelihood ratio, decays by its own half-life, and is scaled by how confident we are that the record actually refers to this operator. The result is capped by evidence quality: no quantity of circumstantial signals can produce a “confirmed” verdict — only a document can do that.

    A verdict of “confirmed” means the operator bought from Sysco as one of its suppliers. It never means everything on the menu is Sysco product. Operators almost always run a primary broadliner alongside produce, protein and specialty houses.

    Think an entry is wrong? Every claim is traceable to a cited source. Corrections and removal requests are honoured on the strength of the record, not the volume of the complaint.
